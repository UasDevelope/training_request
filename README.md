# Training Request App

A Flutter application for booking and managing training sessions, with real-time chat and feedback features.

## Features

- **User Authentication**: Sign up and log in securely with email and password.
- **Book Training Sessions**: Request training by specifying hours, date, and special requirements. Price is auto-calculated.
- **Order Management**: View and manage your bookings/orders in different states (Active, Submitted, In Progress, Completed).
- **Chat**: Real-time chat with drivers/trainers related to your bookings.
- **Feedback**: Rate your training experience and leave comments to help improve the service.
